\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Graph learning on connectomes.}
Message-passing GNNs \citep{kipf2017gcn,velickovic2018gat} are the default model for connectomes,
benchmarked across many datasets by NeuroGraph \citep{said2023neurograph}. For structural
connectomes, \citet{kazi2024multihead} combine edge- and node-centred convolutions in a multi-head
GCN, and \citet{jeong2025gcnptsd} report a pilot GNN study of trauma-exposed youth. Such models are
not additively decomposable in their usual form; post-hoc methods such as GNNExplainer
\citep{ying2019gnnexplainer} optimize masks rather than expose an algebraic prediction sum.

\paragraph{Interpretable-by-design models.}
Generalised additive models \citep{hastie1986gam} write a prediction as a sum of univariate shape
functions; neural additive models \citep{agarwal2021nam} learn them with small networks. GNAN
\citep{bechler2024gnan} carries this to graphs: node features pass through shape functions and
interact through a learned function of \emph{hop distance}, making the model exactly decomposable.
The distance mechanism is designed for binary topology. Our narrower contribution is a monotone
weight-modulated, one-hop interaction with a fixed-graph logit decomposition; we do not claim the
first weighted or interpretable graph model in general, as weighted \citep{kawahara2017brainnetcnn} and interpretable
\citep{li2021braingnn} connectome networks exist, though neither is additively decomposable.

\paragraph{PTSD connectomics and small-sample evaluation.}
Network accounts of PTSD motivate attention to default-mode, salience and central-executive systems
\citep{menon2011triplenetwork,akiki2017network}, and graph studies report disrupted structural
organisation \citep{suo2017smallworld}. Machine-learning studies classify PTSD from such networks
\citep{nicholson2020ptsdml}, but the largest multisite effort finds only modest out-of-sample
accuracy \citep{zhu2023enigma} and data-driven markers remain unestablished
\citep{neria2022datadriven}. With few positives, cross-validated estimates carry large error bars
\citep{varoquaux2018cvfailure} and leakage inflates results \citep{kapoor2023leakage}, which
motivates frozen splits, inner-fold selection, bootstrap intervals and a held-out audit. These prior
findings motivate hypotheses; they are not ground-truth tract biomarkers for our cohort.
